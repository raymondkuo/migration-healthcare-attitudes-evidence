Definition note. 2020: Mainland China (31 provincial-level regions and the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps), National Health Commission: 87,071 confirmed cases and 4,634 deaths reported by 24:00 on 31 December 2020; Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan are not included (WHO's "China" includes them).
Definition note. 2021: Mainland China (31 provincial-level regions and the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps), National Health Commission: 102,314 confirmed cases and 4,636 deaths reported by 24:00 on 31 December 2021, minus those of 31 December 2020; Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan are not included (WHO's "China" includes them).
Definition note. 2022: Mainland China (31 provincial-level regions and the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps), National Health Commission: 397,195 confirmed cases and 5,241 deaths reported by 24:00 on 23 December 2022, minus those of 31 December 2021; Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan are not included (WHO's "China" includes them). INCOMPLETE YEAR: the Commission stopped publishing daily figures on 25 December 2022, so 24 December-31 December are missing; the counts are confirmed cases as the Commission defined them (asymptomatic infections were reported separately and are not included) and miss most of the infections and deaths of the December 2022 wave.
