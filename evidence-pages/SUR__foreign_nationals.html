How it was derived：Sum of the six printed foreign nationality categories of Table H2; the 3,340 unknown-nationality persons are excluded
Definition note. Census count, reference date 13 Aug 2012. Sum of the six foreign nationality categories printed in Table H2: Dutch 10,248 + Guyanese 8,278 + French 3,575 + Brazilian 5,027 + Chinese 3,758 + other 2,167 = 33,053. The 3,340 persons of unknown nationality (Onbekend) are NOT included; adding them gives 36,393, the value held before 2026-10-07. Multiple nationality was not investigated by the census.
How this was confirmed. Sum of the printed Table H2 categories (page 24 of the archived census volume); the table total 541,638 minus Surinamese 505,245 equals the sum plus unknown.
