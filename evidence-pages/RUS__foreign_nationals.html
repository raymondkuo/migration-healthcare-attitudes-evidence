Definition note. Census count; persons who are not citizens of the Russian Federation (about 1% of residents), which the source calls "foreigners". This counts by citizenship, so it includes stateless persons, as the panel's foreign_nationals definition does. Read from page 5 of the archived paper (sentence and Table 1 agree).
