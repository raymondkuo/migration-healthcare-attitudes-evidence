census		Census night, 10 August 2021census count (single year); not stated excluded
Definition note. Census count, not an annual register: persons counted in the 2021 Census of Population and Housing who are not Australian citizens, by place of usual residence, excluding overseas visitors. Of the 25,422,788 persons in the table, 1,307,918 (5.1%) did not state their citizenship and are not in this count; ABS adds small random adjustments to every cell, so the components need not sum to the total exactly. Census night was 10 August 2021. Kept apart from foreign_nationals (an annual series elsewhere); the share in non_citizens_census_pct_pop uses the World Bank population of the panel, like every other share (on the census persons it would be 11.0%).
How this was confirmed. Read directly from the official ABS workbook (non_citizens_census__ABS_Census2021_cultural_diversity_summary__retrieved_2026-10-18.xlsx, sheet Table 2, national Total row; downloaded from https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/people/people-and-communities/cultural-diversity-census/2021/Cultural%20diversity%20data%20summary.xlsx); census date from the archived ABS page non_citizens_census__ABS_about_census__retrieved_2026-10-18.html. Retrieved 2026-10-18.
