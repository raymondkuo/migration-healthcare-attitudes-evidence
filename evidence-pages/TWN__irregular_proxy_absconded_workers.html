Definition note. absconded / missing migrant workers (失聯移工), stock at END OF YEAR 2011. Counts migrant workers whose employment permit has lapsed and who have not been located. This is the Ministry of Labor absconded-worker measure, a DIFFERENT series from the National Immigration Agency 逾期停留/逾期居留 (overstaying visit / overstaying residence) counts used for TWN 2014-2018; the two are not additive and not directly comparable.
