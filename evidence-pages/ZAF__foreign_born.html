Definition note. 2001: Census count; persons born outside South Africa. Read from Figure 3.1 of the Census 2022 Statistical Release, which tabulates Census 1996 = 835,216, 2001 = 1,025,076, 2011 = 2,184,408, 2022 = 2,418,197 on one basis. The 2011 and 2022 values already published are the same figures.
Definition note. 2005: UN estimate (not annual); mid-year benchmark year only; retrieved from World Bank mirror of UN DESA International Migrant Stock, retrieved 2026-10-07.
Definition note. 2010, 2020: UN estimate (not annual); mid-year benchmark year only; retrieved from World Bank mirror of UN DESA International Migrant Stock, accessed 2026-08
Definition note. 2011: Census count, reference date 9-31 Oct 2011; persons born outside South Africa; comparable series in the same table: Census 1996 = 835216, Census 2001 = 1025076
Definition note. 2015: UN estimate (not annual); mid-year benchmark year only; markedly higher than the Stats SA census counts for adjacent years; retrieved from World Bank mirror of UN DESA International Migrant Stock, accessed 2026-08
Definition note. 2016: Large-scale household survey (not a census); persons born outside South Africa; Stats SA itself flags the 2011-2016 decline as anomalous, so treat with caution
Definition note. 2022: Census count, reference night 2-3 Feb 2022 (nearest census to 2021); persons born outside South Africa, just above 3 per cent of the total population; a separate Stats SA Income and Expenditure Survey 2022/23 put the foreign-born population higher at about 3.1 million
How this was confirmed. 2001: Confirmed in the archived source document (page 41, Figure 3.1).
How this was confirmed. 2005: Read directly from the archived API response (evidence/api/wb_SM_POP_TOTL_2001-2022_retrieved_2026-10-07.json); retrieved 2026-10-07.
How this was confirmed. 2011, 2016, 2022: The published figure appears in the archived source document.
