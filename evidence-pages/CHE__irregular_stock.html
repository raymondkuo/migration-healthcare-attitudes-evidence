How it was derived：midpoint of the published range
Published source range：100,000–200,000 (Pew Appendix B, thousands)
Definition note. Expert-interview / Delphi estimate for 12 cantons extrapolated by regression to the remaining 15 cantons, cross-checked against ZEMIS, AHV and vital-statistics data; point estimate 76000, plausible range 50000-99000. Covers third-country (non-EU/EFTA) nationals only.
How this was confirmed. SEM study estimate of 76,000 corroborated by SRF reporting on its release (range 58,000-105,000).
