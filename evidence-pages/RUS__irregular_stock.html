20201,200,000Cnot machine-checkableRussian Ministry of Internal Affairs (MVD) data, reported by The Moscow Times, 16 April 2021See note
Definition note. ADMINISTRATIVE COUNT, not a modelled stock and not an enforcement flow: MVD register of nationals of post-Soviet/CIS states residing in Russia without valid documentation, as at late 2020. LOWER BOUND: source states 'more than 1.2 million'; recorded here as 1,200,000. Covers CIS nationals only (Uzbekistan 332,000; Tajikistan 247,000; Ukraine 152,000; Azerbaijan 120,000; Kyrgyzstan 115,000; Armenia 61,000; Moldova 56,000; Kazakhstan 49,000) and therefore excludes non-CIS irregular migrants. No published method statement accompanies the figure.
