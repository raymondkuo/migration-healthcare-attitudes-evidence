How it was derived：covid_cases / population x 100 (the panel's population of the same country and year)
Definition note. 2020: Taiwan CECC: cumulative laboratory-confirmed cases and cumulative deaths announced on 31 December 2020 (799 cases, 7 deaths); the year's value is the difference. Computed by the archive: 799 cases / population 23,561,236 x 100 (the panel's population of TWN 2020).
Definition note. 2021: Taiwan CECC: cumulative laboratory-confirmed cases and cumulative deaths announced on 31 December 2021 (17,029 cases, 850 deaths), minus those of 31 December 2020; the year's value is the difference. Computed by the archive: 16,230 cases / population 23,375,314 x 100 (the panel's population of TWN 2021).
Definition note. 2022: Taiwan CECC: cumulative laboratory-confirmed cases and cumulative deaths announced on 31 December 2022 (8,847,360 cases, 15,253 deaths), minus those of 31 December 2021; the year's value is the difference. Computed by the archive: 8,830,331 cases / population 23,264,640 x 100 (the panel's population of TWN 2022).
How this was confirmed. 2020: Read from the archived CECC press release of 31 December 2020 (evidence/countries/TWN/covid__CECC_bulletin_2020-12-31__retrieved_2026-11-06.html); the National Health Commission's briefing quotes the same Taiwan totals for 2020 and 2021. Retrieved 2026-11-06.
How this was confirmed. 2021: Read from the archived CECC press release of 31 December 2021 (evidence/countries/TWN/covid__CECC_bulletin_2021-12-31__retrieved_2026-11-06.html) and the one of 31 December 2020; the National Health Commission's briefing quotes the same Taiwan totals for 2020 and 2021. Retrieved 2026-11-06.
