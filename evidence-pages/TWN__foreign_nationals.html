Definition note. Foreign residents holding a valid resident certificate (外僑居留人數), year-end 31 December. INCLUDES migrant workers (移工), who are roughly 75-84% of the total. Series on this basis begins in 2012; 2010 and 2011 are not available on a comparable basis.
