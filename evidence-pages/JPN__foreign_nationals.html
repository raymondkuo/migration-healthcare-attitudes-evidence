Definition note. Registered foreign residents (在留外国人), OECD IMD series sourced from Japan's Immigration Services Agency. Excludes short stays under 90 days. IMPORTANT REFERENCE-DATE CAVEAT (verified): the OECD year label is offset one year later than Japan's ISA 31-December series. ISA reports end-2021 = 2760635 and end-2020 = 2887116 (https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/publications/press/13_00001.html), whereas OECD labels 2887116 as 2021 and 2760635 as 2022. Values are recorded here exactly as OECD publishes them; to align with the ISA national series subtract one from the year label.
