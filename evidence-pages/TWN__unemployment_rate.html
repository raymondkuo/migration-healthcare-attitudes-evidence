Definition note. DGBAS Manpower Survey (人力資源調查): unemployed persons as a share of the labour force, civilian population aged 15 and over (excludes the armed forces and the institutional population), annual average of the monthly survey; published to two decimals (definitions: yearbook PDF page 322). The ILO modelled estimates hold Taiwan as well (data/unemployment_source_comparison.csv): status R (Real value) for 2001-2020, imputed for 2021 and 2022 although DGBAS published those years; the national series is used for every year.
How this was confirmed.
