Definition note. estimated overstayer population (people unlawfully in New Zealand), reference date October 2010. Register-derived estimate from the INZ overstayer estimate series; INZ notes the methodology was revised for the December 2017 estimate, so figures up to October 2014 / January 2016 are on the older basis.
