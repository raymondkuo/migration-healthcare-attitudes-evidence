推導方式：Sum of the six printed foreign nationality categories of Table H2; the 3,340 unknown-nationality persons are excluded
定義說明。Census count, reference date 13 Aug 2012. Sum of the six foreign nationality categories printed in Table H2: Dutch 10,248 + Guyanese 8,278 + French 3,575 + Brazilian 5,027 + Chinese 3,758 + other 2,167 = 33,053. The 3,340 persons of unknown nationality (Onbekend) are NOT included; adding them gives 36,393, the value held before 2026-10-07. Multiple nationality was not investigated by the census.
確認方式。Sum of the printed Table H2 categories (page 24 of the archived census volume); the table total 541,638 minus Surinamese 505,245 equals the sum plus unknown.

本存檔為「民眾對非本國籍人士使用公費醫療之態度」研究之配套資料，供期刊編輯與審查委員查核之用。
