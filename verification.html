Verification record

What was checked, and what changed

Every source was retrieved again on 2026-08-17 and each value in the input workbooks was compared against it. This page reports the result in full, including the values that did not match. Everything done to the data after that day - every correction, deletion, re-classification, re-citation and re-check - is dated in the revision history below. The comparison counts on this page are the records of that first-release check and of the re-check after correction; they are not a count of unique current observations, and a generated evidence page agreeing with the panel is not independent confirmation of how the original source was read. The per-observation disposition of the current panel is in data/current_panel_verification.csv.
